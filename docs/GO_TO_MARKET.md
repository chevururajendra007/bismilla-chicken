# Go-to-Market – Bismilla Chicken app

## Positioning
**"Fresh chicken at today's paper rate – at your door in 30 minutes."**
Tendercuts-style freshness and transparency, Swiggy/Zomato-style convenience, but local: the shop's own prices,
no 20–30 % aggregator commission, and the owner keeps the customer relationship (phone + push).

| | Swiggy / Zomato | Tendercuts / Licious | **Bismilla app** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | Menu + markup + fees | Premium fixed price | **Daily paper rate, shown openly** |
| Delivery in Kota | Limited | Not available | **Free ≤ 3 km** |
| Commission | 18–30 % | – | **0 %** |
| Live / country chicken | No | No | **Yes (Natu Kodi, live bird)** |

## Launch plan (first 30 days)
1. **Week 0 – Shop-ready:** exact branch pins, rate multipliers matched to the board, PhonePe Business QR, SMS OTP live.
2. **Week 1 – In-store capture:** QR standee at both counters: "Scan → install → next order free delivery anywhere in 5 km". Staff installs it for regulars while they wait.
3. **Week 1–2 – WhatsApp:** daily 7 AM status with today's rate + app link; broadcast to the existing customer list.
4. **Week 2 – Sunday push:** Saturday-night push "Sunday special: order by 9 AM, delivered by 11"; pre-orders smooth the Sunday rush.
5. **Week 3 – Local reach:** pamphlets in Vidyanagar colleges / staff quarters; hostels & mess bulk orders (5 kg+).
6. **Week 4 – Referral:** "Share with a friend – both get ₹20 off on 2 kg+".

## Metrics (Admin dashboard)
Orders/day · kg/day · delivery share · repeat customers · % prepaid via PhonePe · average delivery time.

## Pricing to sell the app to other shops (resale)
- Setup ₹4,999 (branding, branches, rates, training) + ₹499/month (hosting, updates, support)
- Optional: SMS pack at cost, custom domain ₹999/yr
- Pitch: "One Swiggy order commission per day pays for the whole app."
